# Nine-Tailed Fox Animation \& Geometry

* **Segmented Rigging**: Each of the 9 tails consists of 12 segments (108 total tail parts) for high-fidelity "Liquid Ripple" movement.
* **Animation Math**: Use Three.js Sine and Cosine wave loops for tail movement.
* **The "Calm" Rule**: Animation speed must be set to "Calm" rather than "Hyper" to maintain an iconic, natural flow.
* **Voxel Hierarchy**: Maintain strict labeling protocols for all 108 segments to ensure the Blender-to-Hytopia export doesn't break the animation bone-mapping.
* **Geometry**: Maintain "Absolute Perfection" on dimensions for the central body primitive to ensure hitbox accuracy.
